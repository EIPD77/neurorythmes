Maintenance: design for benign neglect 🧘🏽‍♀️

Your blog should be fine even if you:

Don’t touch it for 3 months
Don’t update dependencies
Don’t post
Practical tips
Avoid heavy JS frameworks
Avoid comments sections (moderation = stress)
Avoid analytics dashboards (or use simple privacy-friendly ones)
One theme, rarely changed

💡 A silent blog is still a valid blog.
