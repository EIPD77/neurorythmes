Welcome 🌿

This blog is not a performance.  
It is a garden.

## What to Expect

- Posts may be short or long, sometimes structured, sometimes more raw.
- Topics return in cycles (rhythms, thresholds, energy, regulation, design, tools).
- Updates are irregular — this is intentional.

## What You Won’t Find Here

- A promise of frequency (“one post per week”).
- A definitive truth. I am documenting a trajectory, not delivering conclusions.

## How to Use This Blog

- You can read at random: each post is meant to stand on its own.
- You can follow by themes (tags): cycles, systems, accessibility, etc.
- You can come back later: posts are written to remain relevant over time.

## Care Notes

Some posts may touch on sensitive topics. When that’s the case, it is indicated clearly at the top of the page.

Thank you for spending time here 🤍.
